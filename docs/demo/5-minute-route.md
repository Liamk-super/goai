## 0:00–0:35 — Local Demo identity

Open `/demo-login`, enter a 2–40 character nickname, and point out the persistent
“Local Demo Identity — not production authentication” banner. Refresh once to
show browser-session recovery; do not expose storage contents on screen.

## 0:35–1:35 — Dossier intake

Create a project and V1, upload the authorized material, answer the generated
gaps, and confirm the profile. State that uploaded bodies remain private while
the UI and report expose only scoped metadata and evidence hashes.

## 1:35–2:25 — Real asynchronous dispatch

Dispatch the PLANNED Run. Show the frozen Manifest hash, USD 20 hard limit,
RocketMQ Outbox status, AgentTeams v1.2.0 Team, one leader and five specialists.
The HTTP request must already have returned 202; execution continues in the
publisher/bridge processes.

## 2:25–3:25 — Evidence and independent audit

Show four specialist Tasks, at least three distinct Matrix handoffs, a real
browser screenshot Evidence and a real search Evidence. Open the Auditor stage
and show an append-only downgrade or rejection of one insufficient Finding.
Do not describe a fixed fixture or recorded snapshot as live execution.

## 3:25–4:20 — Rule-owned report

Open the report. Cover all four grades, Evidence confidence, V1 comparison,
time/region signal and validity dates, key contradictions, and no more than
three Evidence-linked actions. Explicitly note that there is no 0–100 score or
four-way average and the Leader cannot override the rule result.

## 4:20–5:00 — V2, Ops, and fallback boundary

Open same-standard V2 comparison, point out improved/unchanged/regressed and new
risks, then show the redacted Ops ledger. Finish on `/recorded-snapshot`: its
label is visible and it performs no write. If credentials or authorized URL are
absent, stop here with `BLOCKED_NO_AUTHORIZED_CASE` rather than claiming live E2E.
