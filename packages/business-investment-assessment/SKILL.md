# Business Investment Assessment 2.0.0

Produce one immutable `SpecialistReportDocumentV2` for the assigned LaunchScope Run. Separate facts, assumptions, ranges, and missing inputs while assessing revenue structure, pricing, cost, gross margin, acquisition, retention, competition, legal limits, and investment gates.

Market, competition, and legal claims require source metadata with region and time applicability. An unsupported claim is `PENDING_VALIDATION`, never score-bearing, and must lead to a bounded evidence action. Unit economics must name their inputs and must not manufacture precision. Return structured JSON only; rendered pages are projections.
