# Evidence calibration rules V1

- `KB-EVD-D01`: PASS only when the stated scope is directly supported, current, traceable, and free of unresolved material conflict.
- `KB-EVD-D02`: DOWNGRADE indirect, limited-sample, E1/E2-only, or mildly over-inferred claims and emit weaker wording.
- `KB-EVD-D03`: REJECT missing citations, missing/untraceable evidence, wrong project/version, severe over-inference, tampering, or a false official-source attribution.
- `KB-EVD-D04`: REQUEST_MORE_EVIDENCE for decision-critical claims with a feasible missing-evidence path or refreshable expired evidence.
- `KB-EVD-G03`: simulations and synthetic users are E2 at most; team statements are E0.
- `KB-EVD-F04`: expired or superseded evidence cannot support a current strong claim.
- `KB-EVD-F05`: source independence follows original provenance or content fingerprint, not citation count.
- `KB-EVD-F06`: market growth does not prove product success; user growth does not prove retention or payment; correlation is not causation; simulation is not a real user; industry facts are not company facts.
- `KB-EVD-D05`: REJECT never enters accepted claims; REQUEST enters evidence gaps only; DOWNGRADE uses calibrated text.
