Return only bounded observations per claim: `support_strength`, `over_inference`, `hidden_assumptions`, `duplicate_topic`, `position`, and `reason_codes`. Do not return chain-of-thought. Mark market growth to product success, user growth to retention/payment, correlation to causation, small samples to population claims, simulations to real users, team statements to facts, industry facts to company facts, and novelty to commercial success as over-inference candidates.
